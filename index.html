Quick answers

Before you book.

Why am I paying before submitting the form?

Each package has a dedicated paid-booking form. You'll see the payment instructions first, then enter your transaction reference and payment proof with your academic details. A payment and form submission are not a confirmed appointment.

Can I choose my preferred call time?

Yes. The form asks for two possible dates and times in your selected time zone. These are preferences rather than live free slots. I'll check availability and email your confirmed time and meeting link. If neither works, I'll contact you about an alternative before confirming.

What documents should I prepare?

Bring your current CV if you have one, your degree/CGPA information, and relevant academic records, projects or draft statements. Payment proof is required. A transcript may be helpful, but you should avoid sharing passports, national ID cards, banking credentials or other unnecessary sensitive details.

What if I haven't written a CV or SOP yet?

You can still book. The form lets you explain what you've completed so far. We'll work from your real experiences and the requirements of your target applications.

Will professors definitely reply or will I get a scholarship?

No. University and professor decisions are outside my control. We'll improve clarity, relevance and strategy, but I can't guarantee replies, admissions or funding.

Will you write or submit applications on my behalf?

I'll help structure, edit, review and improve documents collaboratively. I won't invent achievements or submit applications or emails while impersonating you. You retain control over your applications.

What if my requested times are unavailable?

I'll offer another time rather than confirm a slot that doesn't exist. If I can’t offer a suitable alternative time, I’ll refund the fee. Please request any rescheduling at least 24 hours before your confirmed call.
